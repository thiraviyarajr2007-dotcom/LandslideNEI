Scientific Principles

Built for Explainable Decision Support

Disaster risk mitigation cannot rely on opaque black-box statistical models. When lives, evacuation routes, and highway corridors depend on operational alerts, every output must be grounded in geotechnical truth.

visibility

1. Not a Black Box

Full parameter attribution is exposed for every query—slope angle, antecedent rainfall volume, soil plasticity—rather than unverified percentages or opaque predictions.

troubleshoot

2. Data Quality Is Visible

Unavailable sensor telemetry is never silently guessed or set to 0.0 mm. Explicit 'STALE' or 'NO DATA' flags ensure operational integrity in mountain corridors.

rule

3. Susceptibility ≠ Event Probability

Static susceptibility measures intrinsic predisposition to failure under stress. Dynamic operational risk synthesizes live transient precipitation triggers.
